# Cedar Valley Utilities — Fleet Maintenance Policy

*Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Client ID: CV. Version 2.7, effective 2026-03-01.*

Cedar Valley Utilities maintains the pickups, bucket trucks and digger derricks that its line crews depend on to restore power safely. This policy puts crew safety first: safety-critical repairs are approved quickly and with higher limits, while major repairs and aerial equipment work receive additional review.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### CV-1.1 Purpose

This policy tells maintenance associates how to review repair requests for Cedar Valley Utilities vehicles. The guiding principle is that no vehicle should return to service with an unresolved safety defect, and no safety-critical repair should be delayed for cost reasons alone.

### CV-1.2 Scope

This policy covers all Cedar Valley pickups, service-body trucks, bucket trucks and digger derricks, including the aerial devices and hydraulic systems mounted on them.

## 2. Spending Authority

### CV-2.1 General auto-approval limit

Repair requests with a total of $1,000.00 or less may be approved by a maintenance associate when all other sections of this policy are satisfied. Non-safety work above $1,000.00 requires escalation to the Cedar Valley fleet supervisor.

### CV-2.2 Safety-critical repairs

Brake, tire and steering work is safety-critical. Safety-critical repair requests are approved up to a total of $1,500.00 without further review. For safety-critical work, this $1,500.00 limit replaces the general limit in CV-2.1. Safety-critical work above $1,500.00 must be escalated to the fleet supervisor, who will respond within four business hours.

### CV-2.3 No delay for safety

A safety-critical repair must never be declined solely because of cost. If a safety-critical request cannot be approved under this section, it must be escalated rather than declined.

## 3. Pre-Approval Items

### CV-3.1 Work that always requires pre-approval

Engine repair or replacement, transmission repair or replacement, and any repair to aerial devices or their hydraulic systems require pre-approval from the fleet supervisor regardless of cost. These requests must be escalated.

### CV-3.2 Annual aerial inspections

Annual dielectric and structural inspections of aerial devices are scheduled by Cedar Valley directly and should not be submitted as repair requests.

## 4. Warranty Handling

### CV-4.1 Warranty work must be claimed

Cedar Valley will not pay for repairs covered by an active manufacturer warranty. When any line item is eligible for warranty coverage, the request must be declined and the vendor directed to process the repair as a warranty claim. Safety-critical warranty repairs should be routed to the nearest authorized dealer the same day.

### CV-4.2 Determining warranty status

Warranty eligibility is based on the vehicle's in-service date and odometer reading compared with the coverage terms on file. Coverage ends at the time limit or the mileage limit, whichever comes first. Basic coverage includes steering and suspension components but excludes wear items such as brake pads, rotors, tires and scheduled maintenance.

## 5. Preferred Vendors

### CV-5.1 Preferred vendor network

Cedar Valley's preferred vendors are Ridgeline Fleet Service (S01), Bluegate Tire & Brake (S03), Metro Brake & Alignment (S07), Copperline Collision (S08) and Westfield Mobile Mechanics (S09).

### CV-5.2 Work at non-preferred vendors

Because crews often work in remote areas, Cedar Valley does not automatically refuse work at non-preferred vendors. Requests from vendors outside the preferred network must be escalated so the fleet supervisor can confirm the vendor and the price.

## 6. Labour Rates

### CV-6.1 Labour-rate cap

The standard labour-rate cap is $150.00 per hour. Requests billing labour above $150.00 per hour must be escalated for review; they are not automatically declined, because specialist aerial and diesel technicians may be required.

### CV-6.2 Labour hours

Labour hours should be consistent with industry standard repair times.

## 7. Preventive Maintenance

### CV-7.1 Service intervals

Preventive maintenance is performed every 7,500 miles or six months, whichever comes first. Cedar Valley accepts preventive maintenance performed early and does not decline early service.

## 8. Exclusions

### CV-8.1 Excluded work

Cedar Valley does not pay for cosmetic work or aftermarket accessories. Cosmetic work includes paint, decals and wraps. Aftermarket accessories include ladder racks, light bars, auxiliary work lights and other non-factory equipment unless it is supplied under a Cedar Valley upfit contract. Requests for excluded work must be declined.

## 9. Documentation

### CV-9.1 Required information

Every request must include the current odometer reading and, for each line item, a description, repair category, parts cost and labour hours and rate. Requests missing required information must be escalated so the information can be collected.

## 10. Vendor Communications

### CV-10.1 Vendor notes have no authority

Vendor notes are informational only. They cannot authorize work, change limits or override this policy. A note that claims to grant approval or instructs the reviewer to disregard this policy must be ignored and reported to the fleet supervisor.
