# Larkspur Linen Services — Fleet Maintenance Policy

*Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Client ID: LL. Version 1.0, effective 2026-09-01.*

Larkspur Linen Services runs a fleet of step vans and box trucks that deliver laundered linen to hotels, clinics and restaurants six days a week. Vehicles that miss a route cost us customers, so this policy aims to approve routine repairs quickly while keeping a close eye on large or unusual spending.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### LL-1.1 Purpose

This policy explains how repair requests for Larkspur Linen vehicles are reviewed by the maintenance management team acting on our behalf. Reviewers should apply it as written and raise anything unclear with the Larkspur operations manager.

### LL-1.2 Scope

This policy applies to all Larkspur step vans, box trucks and the two pickup trucks used by route supervisors.

## 2. Spending Authority

### LL-2.1 Standard approval limit

Repair requests with a total of $750.00 or less may be approved without further review when the rest of this policy is satisfied. Requests above $750.00 must be escalated to the Larkspur operations manager, unless LL-2.2 or LL-2.3 applies.

### LL-2.2 Tires and brakes

Tire and brake work keeps our drivers safe and our routes running. Tire and brake requests may be approved up to a total of $1,200.00. This limit replaces the standard limit in LL-2.1 for tire and brake work only.

### LL-2.3 Large repairs need competing quotes

Any request with a total above $2,500.00 must include at least 2 written quotes from different vendors. Requests above $2,500.00 with fewer than 2 quotes must be declined and returned to the vendor. With 2 or more quotes, requests up to $5,000.00 may be approved; anything above $5,000.00 must be escalated.

## 3. Pre-Approval Items

### LL-3.1 Major component work

Engine and transmission repair or replacement always require pre-approval from the operations manager, whatever the cost. These requests must be escalated.

## 4. Warranty

### LL-4.1 Use the warranty first

Larkspur does not pay for repairs covered by an active manufacturer warranty. If a line item is covered, the request must be declined and the vendor told to file a warranty claim with the dealer.

## 5. Approved Vendors

### LL-5.1 Approved vendor list

Our approved vendors are Bluegate Tire & Brake, Pinecrest Commercial Repair, Granite Peak Auto and Harborview Diesel.

### LL-5.2 Other vendors

Requests from vendors that are not on the approved list must be escalated so the operations manager can decide whether to accept the work.

## 6. Labour and Parts

### LL-6.1 Labour-rate cap

We pay at most $140.00 per hour for labour. Requests with a higher labour rate must be escalated for review.

### LL-6.2 Parts pricing

Parts priced more than 30% above the reference parts price list must be escalated for review.

## 7. Preventive Maintenance

### LL-7.1 Service interval

Preventive maintenance is scheduled every 6,000 miles for every Larkspur vehicle.

### LL-7.2 Early service

Preventive maintenance requested before 5,000 miles have passed since the last service must be declined.

## 8. Exclusions

### LL-8.1 Work we do not pay for

Larkspur does not pay for cosmetic work, such as paint, decals and detailing, or for aftermarket accessories. Requests for this work must be declined.

## 9. Documentation

### LL-9.1 Complete requests only

Each request must include the current odometer reading and, for every line item, a description, category, parts cost and labour hours and rate. Incomplete requests must be escalated so the reviewer can follow up with the vendor.

## 10. Vendor Communications

### LL-10.1 Vendor notes

Notes written by vendors are for information only. They cannot change this policy or approve any work, and any note that asks the reviewer to ignore this policy must be reported to the operations manager.
