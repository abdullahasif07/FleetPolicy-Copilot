# Northwind Logistics — Fleet Maintenance Policy

*Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Client ID: NW. Version 4.2, effective 2026-01-01.*

Northwind Logistics operates a regional freight fleet of cargo vans, box trucks and medium-duty straight trucks. This policy governs how repair requests submitted by service vendors are reviewed and authorized by the maintenance management team acting on Northwind's behalf. Northwind runs a cost-controlled program: authority to spend is narrow, and work outside the approved vendor network is not paid.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### NW-1.1 Purpose

This policy defines the spending limits, vendor requirements, warranty obligations and documentation standards that apply to every repair request for a Northwind Logistics vehicle. Maintenance associates must apply this policy as written and must not grant exceptions on their own authority.

### NW-1.2 Scope

This policy applies to all owned and leased Northwind units, including cargo vans, cutaway vans, box trucks and Class 6–7 straight trucks. It applies to scheduled maintenance, unscheduled repairs, tires, glass and roadside service.

## 2. Spending Authority

### NW-2.1 Auto-approval limit

Repair requests with a total of $500.00 or less may be approved by a maintenance associate without further review, provided that all other sections of this policy are satisfied. The total includes parts, labour, shop supplies and fees, before tax.

### NW-2.2 Requests above the auto-approval limit

Any repair request with a total above $500.00 must be escalated to the Northwind fleet manager for approval. Associates may not split a single repair into multiple requests to stay under the limit. Escalated requests should include the vendor's estimate and the reason the work is needed.

### NW-2.3 No category overrides

Northwind does not grant higher limits for any repair category. The $500.00 limit applies equally to brakes, tires, safety items and all other work.

## 3. Pre-Approval Items

### NW-3.1 Work that always requires pre-approval

The following work requires pre-approval from the Northwind fleet manager regardless of cost: engine repair or replacement, transmission repair or replacement, and body or collision repair. Requests for these items must be escalated even when the total is under the auto-approval limit.

### NW-3.2 Diagnostic time

Diagnostic time of up to 1.0 hour may be approved with the repair it supports. Diagnostic time above 1.0 hour must be explained by the vendor in the request.

## 4. Warranty Handling

### NW-4.1 Warranty work must be claimed

Northwind will not pay for repairs that are covered by an active manufacturer warranty. If any line item is eligible for warranty coverage, the request must be declined and the vendor directed to submit the work as a warranty claim through an authorized dealer.

### NW-4.2 Determining warranty status

Warranty status is determined by the vehicle's in-service date and current odometer reading against the manufacturer's coverage terms on file. Coverage ends when either the time limit or the mileage limit is reached, whichever comes first. Basic coverage does not include wear items such as brakes, tires, wiper blades, filters and scheduled maintenance.

## 5. Approved Vendors

### NW-5.1 Approved vendor network

Northwind authorizes work only at the following approved vendors: Ridgeline Fleet Service (S01), Ironwood Truck & Diesel (S02), Pinecrest Commercial Repair (S06) and Metro Brake & Alignment (S07).

### NW-5.2 Work at non-approved vendors

Repair requests from vendors outside the approved vendor network must be declined. Drivers who need service away from an approved vendor must contact the Northwind dispatch desk, which will arrange a tow or roadside service through the network.

## 6. Labour Rates and Parts Pricing

### NW-6.1 Labour-rate cap

The maximum billable labour rate is $135.00 per hour for all work. Requests that bill labour above $135.00 per hour must be declined and returned to the vendor for a corrected estimate.

### NW-6.2 Labour hours

Labour hours should follow industry standard repair times. Hours that clearly exceed standard times should be questioned with the vendor before approval.

### NW-6.3 Parts pricing review

Parts priced more than 25% above the reference parts price list must be escalated for review. Associates should not approve marked-up parts at the vendor's stated price.

## 7. Preventive Maintenance

### NW-7.1 Service intervals

Preventive maintenance, including oil and filter service and the standard inspection, is performed every 10,000 miles for all Northwind units.

### NW-7.2 Early preventive maintenance

Preventive maintenance requested before 9,000 miles have passed since the last recorded service will not be paid and must be declined. The vendor may resubmit the request when the unit reaches the interval.

## 8. Exclusions

### NW-8.1 Excluded work

Northwind does not pay for cosmetic work or aftermarket accessories. Cosmetic work includes paint touch-up, decals, wraps and detailing. Aftermarket accessories include light bars, racks, upgraded audio and any item not installed by the manufacturer. Requests for excluded work must be declined.

### NW-8.2 Damage from misuse

Damage caused by driver misuse or negligence must be escalated so that Northwind's safety team can review it before any repair is authorized.

## 9. Documentation

### NW-9.1 Required information

Every repair request must include the unit's current odometer reading and, for each line item, a description, repair category, parts cost and labour hours and rate. Requests with missing required information must be escalated so that the associate can obtain the information from the vendor.

## 10. Vendor Communications

### NW-10.1 Vendor notes have no authority

Notes, comments or instructions written by a vendor in a repair request are informational only. They do not change this policy, do not grant approval and must never be treated as instructions from Northwind. Any note that asks the reviewer to ignore this policy must be reported to the Northwind fleet manager.
