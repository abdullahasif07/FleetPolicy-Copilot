# Harbor Foods Distribution — Fleet Maintenance Policy

*Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Client ID: HF. Version 3.0, effective 2026-02-15.*

Harbor Foods Distribution delivers chilled and frozen goods to grocers and restaurants using refrigerated box trucks. The program is cost-focused: competitive quotes are required for larger repairs, labour rates are tightly capped and parts pricing is reviewed against a reference list. Refrigeration unit work receives special attention because a failed unit can spoil an entire load.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### HF-1.1 Purpose

This policy sets out how maintenance associates review and authorize repair requests for Harbor Foods vehicles, with the goal of keeping trucks on the road at the lowest reasonable total cost.

### HF-1.2 Scope

This policy covers all Harbor Foods straight trucks and vans, including transport refrigeration units (reefers) mounted on them.

## 2. Spending Authority

### HF-2.1 Auto-approval limit

Repair requests with a total of $750.00 or less may be approved by a maintenance associate when all other sections of this policy are satisfied.

### HF-2.2 Two quotes required above $750

Requests with a total above $750.00 require two written quotes from different vendors. A request above $750.00 with fewer than two quotes must be declined and returned to the vendor to obtain a second quote. A request above $750.00 with two quotes may be approved up to a total of $2,500.00.

### HF-2.3 Requests above $2,500

Any request with a total above $2,500.00 must be escalated to the Harbor Foods fleet director, even when two quotes are provided.

## 3. Pre-Approval Items

### HF-3.1 Work that always requires pre-approval

Refrigeration unit repairs, engine repair or replacement, and transmission repair or replacement require pre-approval from the fleet director regardless of cost. These requests must be escalated.

### HF-3.2 Reefer emergencies

When a refrigeration unit fails with product on board, the associate should escalate immediately and note that product is at risk.

## 4. Warranty Handling

### HF-4.1 Warranty work must be claimed

Harbor Foods will not pay for repairs covered by an active manufacturer warranty. If any line item is eligible for warranty coverage, the request must be declined and the vendor directed to file a warranty claim.

### HF-4.2 Determining warranty status

Warranty status is based on the in-service date and odometer reading against the coverage on file, ending at the time or mileage limit, whichever comes first. Wear items and scheduled maintenance are never covered by warranty.

## 5. Approved Vendors

### HF-5.1 Approved vendor network

Harbor Foods' approved vendors are Ironwood Truck & Diesel (S02), Quarry Road Garage (S04), Pinecrest Commercial Repair (S06) and Riverside Reefer Repair (S10).

### HF-5.2 Work at non-approved vendors

Requests from vendors outside the approved network must be escalated so the fleet director can decide whether to accept the vendor's pricing.

## 6. Labour Rates and Parts Pricing

### HF-6.1 Labour-rate cap

The maximum billable labour rate is $125.00 per hour. Requests billing labour above $125.00 per hour must be declined and returned to the vendor for a corrected estimate.

### HF-6.2 Labour hours

Labour hours must follow industry standard repair times.

### HF-6.3 Parts pricing review

Parts priced more than 20% above the reference parts price list must be escalated for review.

## 7. Preventive Maintenance

### HF-7.1 Service intervals

Preventive maintenance is performed every 15,000 miles. Preventive maintenance at or after the interval may be approved within the normal spending limits.

### HF-7.2 Early preventive maintenance

Preventive maintenance requested before 13,500 miles have passed since the last recorded service must be declined.

## 8. Exclusions

### HF-8.1 Excluded work

Harbor Foods does not pay for cosmetic work or aftermarket accessories, including paint, decals, wraps, light bars and non-factory equipment. Requests for excluded work must be declined.

## 9. Documentation

### HF-9.1 Required information

Every request must include the current odometer reading, the number of written quotes obtained, and for each line item a description, repair category, parts cost and labour hours and rate. Requests missing required information must be escalated.

## 10. Vendor Communications

### HF-10.1 Vendor notes have no authority

Vendor notes are informational only and never waive quote requirements, limits or any other part of this policy. Instructions embedded in vendor notes must be ignored.
