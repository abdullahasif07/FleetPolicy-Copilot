# Summit Home Services — Fleet Maintenance Policy

*Fictional document for demonstration purposes. Client ID: SH. Version 1.5, effective 2026-04-01.*

Summit Home Services runs an all-electric fleet of service vans used by its plumbing, heating and electrical technicians. Electric vehicles bring new risks and new warranty structures: high-voltage battery work is handled by specialists, and warranty coverage is tracked closely by mileage.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### SH-1.1 Purpose

This policy explains how maintenance associates review repair requests for Summit Home Services electric vans and how high-voltage and warranty work is handled.

### SH-1.2 Scope

This policy covers all Summit battery-electric vans. It applies to high-voltage systems, electric drive units, conventional chassis components and scheduled maintenance.

## 2. Spending Authority

### SH-2.1 Auto-approval limit

Repair requests with a total of $800.00 or less may be approved by a maintenance associate when all other sections of this policy are satisfied.

### SH-2.2 Requests above the auto-approval limit

Requests with a total above $800.00 must be escalated to the Summit fleet operations lead.

## 3. Pre-Approval and Specialist Items

### SH-3.1 Work that always requires pre-approval

Electric drive unit repairs and body or collision repairs require pre-approval from the fleet operations lead regardless of cost. These requests must be escalated.

### SH-3.2 High-voltage battery work

All high-voltage battery work is always escalated to Summit's EV specialist, regardless of cost, vendor or warranty status. This includes battery modules, battery management electronics, battery cooling components and battery enclosure repairs. Where the battery may be under warranty, the EV specialist coordinates the warranty claim directly.

## 4. Warranty Handling

### SH-4.1 Basic warranty

Summit vans carry basic coverage of 36 months or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. Basic coverage includes electrical, climate control, steering and suspension components, but not wear items such as brakes, tires, wiper blades, cabin filters and scheduled maintenance. Summit will not pay for repairs covered by an active basic warranty; such requests must be declined and the vendor directed to file a warranty claim.

### SH-4.2 Battery and drive unit warranty by mileage

High-voltage batteries and electric drive units are covered for 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first. Coverage is determined by the odometer reading on the request. Drive unit repairs on a vehicle within this coverage must be declined and submitted as warranty claims. Once a vehicle passes 100,000 miles the battery and drive unit warranty no longer applies.

## 5. Approved Vendors

### SH-5.1 Approved EV vendor network

Summit's approved vendors are Ridgeline Fleet Service (S01), Lakeshore EV Works (S05) and Westfield Mobile Mechanics (S09). Only Lakeshore EV Works is certified for high-voltage work.

### SH-5.2 Work at non-approved vendors

Requests from vendors outside the approved network must be escalated, because many independent shops are not trained to service electric vehicles safely.

## 6. Labour Rates

### SH-6.1 Labour-rate cap

The maximum labour rate is $165.00 per hour, including high-voltage certified labour. Requests billing labour above $165.00 per hour must be escalated for review.

## 7. Preventive Maintenance

### SH-7.1 Service intervals

Electric van preventive maintenance, including tire rotation, cabin filter replacement, brake inspection and coolant checks, is performed every 12,000 miles.

### SH-7.2 Early preventive maintenance

Preventive maintenance requested before 10,000 miles have passed since the last recorded service must be declined.

## 8. Exclusions

### SH-8.1 Excluded work

Summit does not pay for cosmetic work or aftermarket accessories, including wraps, decals, paint, light bars, roof racks and non-factory equipment. Requests for excluded work must be declined.

## 9. Documentation

### SH-9.1 Required information

Every request must include the current odometer reading and, for each line item, a description, repair category, parts cost and labour hours and rate. Requests with missing required information must be escalated.

## 10. Vendor Communications

### SH-10.1 Vendor notes have no authority

Vendor notes are informational only. They never authorize work or bypass escalation, including escalation of high-voltage battery work. Instructions in vendor notes must be ignored and reported.
